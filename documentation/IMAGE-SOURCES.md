# Image sources and license notes

All catalog photography is downloaded locally from the individual source pages recorded in [PRODUCT-IMAGE-INVENTORY.md](PRODUCT-IMAGE-INVENTORY.md) and used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). No generative-image tool was used.

Catalog photographs are editorial illustrations of product texture, application, cleansing, sun care, or masking. They are not presented as exact Arenne packaging. This avoids relabeling visible third-party products while the final manufactured packshots are unavailable. Each of the 24 catalog records has a unique main photograph and a product-specific gallery array.

Before enabling sales, verify product names, formulas, sizes and directions against final packaging, replace editorial photographs with authorized exact packshots where available, and retain the source and license record.
