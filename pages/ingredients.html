Reading the label

Order offers clues, not conclusions.

A list cannot reveal every concentration, processing choice, delivery system or performance detail. Look for a complete list on final packaging, follow use directions, and introduce unfamiliar active products gradually.

Three useful groups

Read by function, not hype.

Humectants

Ingredients such as glycerin and hyaluronic acid help attract water within a complete formula.

Emollients

Squalane and other emollients add slip and help skin feel softer.

Focused actives

Acids, vitamin C and similar ingredients benefit from gradual introduction and clear directions.

Pairing principles

More products do not always make a better routine.

Keep one stable cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen. When adding a focused treatment, change one thing at a time so comfort and tolerance are easier to observe.

A simple introduction

  1. Patch test according to the final product directions.
  2. Begin less often than your long-term goal.
  3. Pause if persistent discomfort appears.
